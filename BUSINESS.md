# BareVial — Sourcing & Launch Playbook

## 1. Vendor sourcing (run in this order)

**Target landed cost:** $6–14/vial on hero SKUs. Walk away above $16.

### Where to look
- Alibaba → search `peptide`, filter **Verified Supplier + Trade Assurance**, sort by orders. Message 20, keep the 5 that answer in <24h.
- Made-in-China → cross-check the same company names for pricing.
- US warehouses / domestic wholesale for hero SKUs only (faster ship, higher cost).

### Vetting checklist (reject on any miss)
- [ ] Trade Assurance active, 3+ yrs, response rate >90%
- [ ] Will **blind-ship** (no supplier name on pack)
- [ ] Will **private-label** vials/boxes with BareVial art
- [ ] Has **US warehouse** + dropship API or daily CSV/email feed
- [ ] Sends **batch COA** (HPLC + LC-MS) per lot, lot number matches vial
- [ ] Sample order arrives — verify purity + label quality before first buy
- [ ] Two suppliers confirmed per hero SKU (no single point of failure)

### Outreach message (paste, tweak name)
> Hi — I run a peptide storefront doing dropship at volume. Need: US-warehouse dropship, blind ship, private-label vials (my artwork), batch COA per order. First order 50 vials across 5 SKUs, monthly reorder. Can you do white-label + dropship and send your price list + a sample COA?

### Red flags
No COA, MOQ 100+, no US stock, vague purity claims, invoice-only (no Trade Assurance).

---

## 2. Listing rules
- Sell as **Research Use Only**. No disease claims. Sitewide RUO footer.
- Purity + batch number on every product page. COA one tap away.
- Price 20–30% under market on the hero SKU; margin back via stacks + membership.

---

## 3. X engine (runs daily)

**Bio:** `Research-grade peptides. COA on every batch. Ships 24h. Bare pricing. RUO. → link`
**Pinned:** live price list image, updated weekly. Link in the *first reply*, never the post.

### Daily cadence
- 09:00 — **price post**: `Reta 10mg — $34. Batch RV-R10-2604, 99.4% (COA in replies). Ships 24h.` + vial/COA image
- 12:00 — **price-check** (Mon/Wed/Fri): screenshot competitor $79 vs your $34
- 15:00 — **proof post** (Tue/Thu): COA close-up or pack-out photo
- 18:00 — **stack / member** post (Sun): bundle math, "price-locked forever"
- **Comments:** watch 20 big peptide/biohacker accounts, reply <5 min, lead with info (batch date, purity), then the price delta.

### Reply variants (rotate; never spam the same line)
1. `we run Reta 10mg at $34, batch RV-R10-2604 99.4%, COA on request. ships 24h.`
2. `$34 over here — same mg, COA-backed. and we price-match + beat by $5.`
3. `our Reta is $34 with a matching batch COA, not just a label. US warehouse, 24h.`
4. `cheaper than that — $34. verify the COA yourself, batch number's in the reply.`
5. `$34 here. members lock that price forever at 15% under it.`
6. `same compound, half the markup. $34, COA in the box, ships tomorrow.`

### Weekly
Refresh pinned price list · post 1 stack deal · reply-bomb any "where do I buy" thread · DM every engager a 1-line + link.
