Policy

Research Use Only

Everything BareVial sells is a research chemical. This page explains what that means in practice, so expectations are clear before you order.

What RUO means

“Research Use Only” marks compounds that are supplied for laboratory and in-vitro research rather than as licensed medicines. They have not gone through the FDA drug-approval pathway. That is exactly why they are available without a prescription — and why they are cheaper.

What it does not mean

  • Not a medicine, and not FDA-approved.
  • Not intended for human or veterinary consumption.
  • Dosing, cycles, and protocol notes on this site are guidance, not medical advice.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for how you store, handle, reconstitute, and use the material, and for confirming that its research use is lawful where you are. Where a certificate is provided, it certifies the tested batch, not your handling.

Quality standard

Every batch is third-party tested by HPLC for purity and LC-MS for identity, and carries a lot-matched Certificate of Analysis. Typical purity is 99%+.

Shipping

Vials are lyophilized (powder), which is stable at ambient temperature for transit. Refrigerate or freeze on arrival for long-term storage; keep dry and away from light.
