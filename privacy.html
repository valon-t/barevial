Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what we collect and why. We keep it to what an order actually needs.

What we collect

  • Order data — name, shipping address, email, and order contents, to fulfil and support your order.
  • Payment data — handled by our payment processors; we never store full card numbers.
  • Usage data — anonymous page views and basic device info, to improve the store.
  • Cart — stored in your browser's local storage, not on our servers, until you order.

How we use it

To process and ship orders, send order updates, handle support and returns, prevent fraud, and measure what's working. We do not sell your data.

Who we share it with

Only the parties needed to deliver the order: our fulfilment partner (name and address for shipping), payment processor, and courier. Each handles data under their own policies.

Retention

Order records are kept as long as needed for tax and accounting, then deleted.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting us. Where applicable (e.g. GDPR/CCPA), we honour those rights within statutory timeframes.

Cookies

We use only functional storage needed for the cart and basic analytics. No advertising cookies are set by default.

Contact

Use the contact link in the footer for any privacy request.
